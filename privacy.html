Privacy policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

Short version: Summoner's Quiz has no accounts and does not ask for personal data. It counts visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which uses no cookies and does not identify you. Your game progress stays in your own browser. The game pages (one per category) show ads from Google AdSense; in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland they load only after you agree. The start page and the information pages (how to play, about, this page) show no ads and load nothing from Google.

What is saved on your device

To remember your answers, finished days and streak, the game uses your browser's local storage (keys that start with dailyleague). This data never leaves your device and we cannot see it. It is needed for the game to work, so no consent banner is shown for it. You can delete it at any time by clearing the site data in your browser settings; that also resets your streak.

On the game pages only, Google's consent message saves your choice about ads in a cookie, so it does not ask again on every visit. If you agree to ads, Google and its advertising partners may also store and read cookies or similar identifiers on your device, as described under Advertising.

What we collect

We do not collect, store or sell any personal data ourselves. There is no sign-up, no contact form and no newsletter. If you email us, we receive your email address and your message and use them only to answer you. On the game pages, Google receives data to show ads; see Advertising.

Visit counts. Every page loads Cloudflare Web Analytics, so we can see how many people visit and which pages they open. It stores nothing on your device (no cookies, no local storage) and does not track you across visits or across sites. When a page loads, Cloudflare receives the page address, the referring page, your browser and device type, your country and page load times; your IP address is used to deliver the request but, according to Cloudflare, not to identify or count you. We see only totals. This is based on our legitimate interest in knowing how the site is used (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR); an ad or content blocker stops it, and the game works the same. See Cloudflare Web Analytics and the Cloudflare privacy policy.

Third parties your browser contacts

Like any website, loading a page means your browser makes requests, and the servers that answer can see your IP address and basic browser details. This happens with:

  • GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.), which hosts this site. See the GitHub privacy statement.
  • Cloudflare Web Analytics (Cloudflare, Inc.), on every page (cloudflareinsights.com), which counts visits. See Visit counts.
  • Buy Me a Coffee, only if you click the coffee button. Nothing is sent to them before you click.
  • Google, on the game pages only: Google's consent message (fundingchoicesmessages.google.com) and, depending on your choice, Google AdSense (pagead2.googlesyndication.com and other Google ad servers). See Advertising.

The site's fonts (Inter and Spectral) and all its images (champion, item and ability icons, crests, team logos and player photos) are served from this site itself, so no font or image service is contacted. We do not control how the services above handle data; please see their own privacy policies.

Advertising

The game is free and paid for by ads. They are served by Google AdSense, a service of Google Ireland Limited (Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland) for visitors in the European Economic Area and Switzerland, and of Google LLC (1600 Amphitheatre Parkway, Mountain View, CA 94043, USA) for everyone else. Ads appear only on the game pages, as one banner: left of the game on wide screens, below the game on phones and other narrow screens. No other page of this site shows ads or loads Google's ad code.

Asking first. Before any ad is loaded, a game page loads Google's consent message, a consent management platform certified under the IAB Transparency and Consent Framework. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland it asks whether Google and its advertising partners may store and read information on your device and use it for ads. Until you agree, no ad is loaded. If you decline, the game works exactly the same, just without ads. Loading the consent message itself sends Google your IP address and basic browser details, and saving your choice uses a cookie; both are needed to ask you at all. Outside those regions ads load without a consent question, and you can opt out of personalised ads with the links below.

What Google receives. When ads load, Google receives your IP address, your browser and device details, the address of the page and, with your consent, cookies or similar identifiers. Google uses them to choose, show and measure ads, to prevent fraud and, if you allowed it, to show ads based on your interests. Advertising partners that you allow in the consent message may receive data as well; the message lists them. See how Google uses information from sites that use its services, how Google uses cookies in advertising and the Google privacy policy. Google may process data in the United States; Google LLC is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

Legal basis. Storing and reading information on your device for ads, and the ad processing that follows, are based on your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, § 25(1) TDDDG). Loading the consent message and remembering your choice are necessary to ask for that consent and to respect it (Art. 6(1)(c) and (f) GDPR, § 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG).

Changing your mind. You can withdraw or change your consent at any time, as easily as you gave it: use the Privacy and cookie settings link at the bottom of any game page, which opens the consent message again. Withdrawing does not affect what happened before. You can also manage personalised ads in Google's My Ad Center, at youronlinechoices.eu or at optout.aboutads.info, block cookies in your browser, or use an ad blocker; the game works without ads.

Children

The game is general-audience trivia and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Your rights and contact

Apart from emails you send us, we hold no personal data about you (the visit counts are totals only), so there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete; you control the game data, in your browser. The data Google receives for ads is held by Google, and you can use your rights with Google directly (see the Google privacy policy above).

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct and delete your personal data, to restrict or object to its processing, to data portability, and to withdraw a consent at any time. You also have the right to complain to a data protection supervisory authority, in particular in the country where you live.
