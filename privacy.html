Privacy policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

Short version: Summoner's Quiz has no accounts, does not ask for personal data and does not use analytics, tracking or advertising cookies. Your game progress stays in your own browser.

What is saved on your device

To remember your answers, finished days and streak, the game uses your browser's local storage (keys that start with dailyleague). This data never leaves your device and we cannot see it. It is needed for the game to work, so no consent banner is shown for it. You can delete it at any time by clearing the site data in your browser settings; that also resets your streak.

What we collect

We do not collect, store or sell any personal data. There is no sign-up, no contact form, no newsletter and no analytics service on this site.

Third parties your browser contacts

Like any website, loading a page means your browser makes requests, and the servers that answer can see your IP address and basic browser details. This happens with:

  • GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.), which hosts this site. See the GitHub privacy statement.
  • Riot Games' Data Dragon (ddragon.leagueoflegends.com), which serves the champion and item icons.
  • The League of Legends Wiki (wiki.leagueoflegends.com), which serves region and faction crests in some lore questions.
  • Buy Me a Coffee, only if you click the coffee button. Nothing is sent to them before you click.

The site uses your system's fonts, so no web font is downloaded from anyone. We do not control how these services handle data; please see their own privacy policies.

Advertising

The site does not show advertising at the moment. If that changes, this policy will be updated before it does, and where the law requires it you will be asked for consent first.

Children

The game is general-audience trivia and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.
