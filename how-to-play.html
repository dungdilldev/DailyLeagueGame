How to play

Summoner's Quiz is a free League of Legends trivia game with a new puzzle every day. Everyone gets the same puzzle, so you can compare your result with friends.

The daily puzzle

  • Each puzzle has 5 rounds.
  • Every round shows 4 options (champions, items, abilities, teams, places and so on) and one question. Tap the option you think is right.
  • Your answer is final. The right option turns green; if you picked a wrong one it turns red. For questions about numbers, such as base health or number of skins, the values are shown after you answer.
  • The rounds get harder: the first two are easy, the third is medium and the last two are hard. In the hard rounds the best and second-best option are close together.
  • After round 5 you see your score out of 5 and can copy a result to share. The shared text only has coloured squares, so it gives nothing away.

Kinds of questions

  • Trivia: the game itself and how it was made. Scrapped champions and what they became, removed maps, items and summoner spells, voice line easter eggs, the music bands, ranked history and Riot's spin-off games.
  • Lore: the world of Runeterra. Which champion belongs to a region, faction or event, and where its places lie: which champion belongs to the Shadow Isles, which sea the Great City of Demacia overlooks, who fought in the Noxian invasion of Ionia.
  • Stats: numbers from the game itself. Which champion, item or ability has the highest value of something, for example release date, base stats, skin count, gold efficiency, ability damage, AP ratio or cooldown: which champion has the most skins, which item gives the most ability power, which ultimate deals the most damage. Ability numbers are at max rank.
  • Esports: fifteen years of competitive League. World Championship and Mid-Season Invitational winners and finals, famous players and their teams, team logos and esports skins.
  • Mixed: a bit of everything, trivia, lore, stats and esports questions in one puzzle. The best test of all-round knowledge.

Pick a mode on the start page. The links at the top of the game switch between them. Each mode has its own puzzle, its own saved progress and its own streak.

Where the questions come from

Stat questions use Riot Games' official Data Dragon and the League of Legends Wiki (release dates, item gold values, ability numbers at max rank, patch history). The data is refreshed automatically every day, so new champions, reworks and balance changes show up in the puzzle without waiting for an update. Lore questions are written by hand from the wiki's pages on the regions and events of Runeterra, and esports questions from the history of the World Championship and the Mid-Season Invitational. Trivia questions are written by hand from the wiki's pages on unreleased content, removed features, champion audio and Riot Games. More on the sources on the about page.

New puzzle and streaks

  • A new puzzle appears every day at midnight UTC. After you finish, a countdown shows when the next one is out.
  • Your streak counts the days in a row that you finished the puzzle. If you haven't played yet today your streak is still alive; it resets once a whole day has been missed.
  • You can also play the previous 7 days with the day buttons above the game. Those days do not count towards your streak.

Good to know

  • The game is free and there are no accounts.
  • Your progress is saved in your browser, so you can close the page and continue later on the same device. See the privacy policy.
  • Clearing your browser data also clears your progress and streak. Another device or browser starts from zero.
