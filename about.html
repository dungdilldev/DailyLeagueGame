About Summoner's Quiz

Summoner's Quiz is a free daily trivia game for fans of League of Legends. Every day there are five rounds, each with four options and one question. It is a small independent hobby project with no accounts and nothing to install. See how to play for the rules.

Where the questions come from

The puzzle is built in your browser from the date, so every player sees the same one. The data behind it is refreshed automatically every day, so new champions and patches show up without anyone editing the game.

  • Champion and item data (names, icons, base stats, skins, titles) comes from Riot Games' official Data Dragon.
  • Release dates, item gold values and patch history come from the League of Legends Wiki, licensed under CC BY-SA 3.0.
  • Lore questions are written by hand and checked against the pages of the League of Legends Wiki about regions and events.
  • Esports questions use the history of the World Championship from Wikipedia, and team logos and player photos from Leaguepedia.
